# How to Make the Perfect Cup of Coffee

This document will walk you through the basic steps to brew coffee using a French Press.

## Prerequisites
* Freshly ground coffee beans
* Hot water (around 200°F)
* A French press

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Measure your coffee:** Add 1 tablespoon of coffee grounds per cup.
2. **Add water:** Pour hot water over the grounds, filling the press.
3. **Wait:** Let it steep for exactly 4 minutes.
4. **Plunge:** Press the plunger down slowly.
